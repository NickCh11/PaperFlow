PaperFlow helps you scan, edit and organize paper documents on your Android device.

• Scan documents and book spreads, crop pages and adjust their appearance.
• Create PDFs, including searchable PDFs using offline OCR.
• Recognize text and export it to Word documents.
• Translate images and PDF pages on your device with downloaded language packs.
• Retake individual pages before saving.

OCR and translation require language packs downloaded from the offline-translator model host. Document images and recognized text are processed locally. Internet access is used for language downloads and update checks.

Android 8.0 or later is required. PDF translation accepts up to 100 pages and 256 MiB per input. Password-protected PDFs require an unlocked copy.
